I design open systems for optical data storage, spectral encoding and custom compute — from instruction set and compiler through simulator, tooling and education.

The public work under @Janus5G is ChromaPlex and PRISME.

PRISME stores data in five spectral channels in glass: ten bits per light pulse, UV error checking, zero power at rest. ChromaPlex is an experimental CPL/CPA stack with compiler, assembler and crystal-voxel simulator. Around that sits a Linux-first editor, an ISO workbench, portable binary interchange and a multi-mode coding agent. ChromaLearn is an open AI learning platform for schools that keeps the teacher in control and the session private.

Sponsorship pays for time on those public repositories — builds, documentation, experiments and maintenance. It does not buy research direction, equity, tokens or exclusive rights.
